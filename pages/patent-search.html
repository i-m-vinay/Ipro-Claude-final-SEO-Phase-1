Why Prior Art Search Matters

Three Searches Every Inventor Needs

🔍 Patentability Search

Determines whether your invention is novel and non-obvious over existing prior art. Essential before filing a provisional or complete application.

⚖️ Freedom-to-Operate (FTO) Search

Checks whether commercialising your product will infringe any third-party patent claims. Critical for startups before product launch or fundraising.

🗺️ Landscape Search

Maps the entire patent landscape in your technology area — competitors, filing trends, white spaces and licensing opportunities.
