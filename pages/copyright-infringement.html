Your Legal Rights

What Counts as Copyright Infringement?

Under the Copyright Act, 1957, infringement occurs when any person reproduces, communicates, adapts or distributes a copyrighted work without the owner's authorisation. Common infringements include:

Copying software code or features without licence
Republishing books, articles or educational material
Using music or film without a sync or broadcast licence
Reproducing logos, illustrations or photographs
Creating derivative works without authorisation
Selling or distributing pirated digital content
