Why Renewal Matters

What Happens If You Don't Renew?

A registered trademark in India is valid for 10 years from the date of application. After that, it must be renewed — or it lapses into the public domain. Once lapsed:

Competitors can apply for your brand name
You lose the right to use the ® symbol
Re-registration from scratch takes 2+ years
Court cases become much harder to win

You can renew up to 6 months before expiry — or up to 12 months after expiry with a surcharge. We track your deadline and file before it becomes urgent.
