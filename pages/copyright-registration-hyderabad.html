Copyright Registration in Hyderabad — Common Questions

Copyright registration in India covers literary works (books, articles, code), artistic works (logos, illustrations, photographs), musical works, sound recordings, films and software. Businesses and creators in Hyderabad can register any of these with the Copyright Office of India.
Copyright protection exists automatically upon creation under the Copyright Act, 1957. However, registration creates an official public record that is admissible as evidence in court. For businesses in Hyderabad that may need to enforce their rights, registration is strongly recommended.
