Patent Filing in Mumbai — Common Questions

Under the Patents Act, 1970, any invention that is novel, involves an inventive step and is capable of industrial application can be patented. Mumbai-based companies commonly file patents for mechanical innovations, chemical processes, pharmaceutical formulations, electronic devices and software-implemented inventions.
