Understanding Your Objection

What Is a Trademark Objection?

After you file a trademark application, the Trade Marks Registry examines it and may raise an Examination Report — also called a trademark objection. This is not a rejection. It is a formal opportunity to defend your mark.

Common reasons for objection include:

Similarity to an existing registered mark
Descriptiveness — mark describes the goods/services
Geographic name or common surname
Lack of distinctiveness
Incorrect classification of goods/services

You have 30 days from the date of examination report to file a reply. Missing this deadline can result in abandonment of your application.
